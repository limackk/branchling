# Code of conduct

## Expected conduct

Be precise, respectful and constructive. Discuss the work rather than the
person doing it, make room for different levels of experience, and accept a
clear technical or scope decision without turning disagreement into personal
conflict. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualised attention, deliberate
intimidation and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews and
other project-managed discussion spaces. It also applies when somebody
represents the project in a public forum. It does not claim authority over
unrelated private communication or spaces the maintainer does not control.

## Reporting and enforcement

The repository maintainer,
[@limackk](https://github.com/limackk), is responsible for enforcement. A
non-sensitive concern may be flagged in the relevant thread. For a confidential
report, use
[the repository's private reporting form](https://github.com/limackk/branchling/security/advisories/new)
and begin the summary with `Conduct:`. Do not put private or identifying details
in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, close a discussion, reject a
contribution, warn a participant, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. The action will be proportionate to the conduct, its impact and
whether it is repeated. Platform-wide abuse may also be reported through
[GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Reports are handled as maintainer availability permits. No response-time or
confidentiality guarantee beyond the privacy provided by GitHub's reporting
form is made.
